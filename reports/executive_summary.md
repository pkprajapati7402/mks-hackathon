# 🏛️ Executive Summary: Celeris Automobili Demand Control Tower

## Strategic Outlook (2026-11 to 2028-05)

- **Total 19-Month Projected Portfolio Demand**: **2,748,978 units** across **595 SKUs** and **6 global commercial regions**.
- **Historical Comparison**: Total unconstrained demand over 2019-2026 was **11,628,180 units**.
- **Technology Transition**: Electric Vehicle (EV) assembly demand is projected to expand significantly, driven by accelerating global adoption indices, while internal combustion (ICE) components plateau.

## Core Operational Recommendations for Supply Chain Leadership

1. **Transition from Constrained Shipments to True Unconstrained Demand**: Utilizing stockout-reconstructed demand prevents historical logistics bottlenecks from depressing future production allocations.
2. **P90 Buffer Procurement for Critical Powertrain & Braking Lines**: Procure raw materials and secure long-lead tier-2 semiconductor capacity against P90 quantile projections to avoid repeating the 2021 component shortages.
3. **Synchronize EOL Phase-Outs with Predecessor Successor SKUs**: Execute proactive ramp-downs 3 months prior to announced EOL dates, shifting customer allocations smoothly to successor components mapped via `Replaces_SKU`.
4. **Exploit Macroeconomic Lead Indicators**: Utilize `New_Vehicle_Orders_Index` (which leads Powertrain orders by 3 months) to adjust assembly shifts before orders arrive in the ERP backlog.
